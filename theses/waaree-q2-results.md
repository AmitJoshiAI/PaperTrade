## Why (the long form)

Waaree Energies is the most talked-about solar name in India since its October 2024 IPO (76x
subscribed, record 97 lakh applications, listed at a 70% premium). The lesson from that listing and
from its recent quarters is that the stock trades on expectations, not on the print: Q2 FY26 profit
rose about 133% and Q1 FY27 revenue rose 79%, and the stock slipped about 6% after the Q1 FY27 numbers.

This thesis does not predict the results. It is a deliberately small (Rs 50,000, 10% cap) test of a
pre-written rule: hold into the event, sell the next open no matter what. The point is to measure what
a "positioned before the catalyst, out the day after" trade earns after charges, on a stock whose
results day is widely watched.

Price at writing (close 15 Oct 2026): Rs 2,494. Order queued with decision_date 2026-10-16, so it
fills at the first open after that date.

## What would change my mind

- The board meeting is set for a date after 2026-10-28: the rule still applies on the first trading
  day after event_date (sell), so the test is simply cut short. Update event_date only if the
  announcement comes BEFORE the 28th.
- A pre-results guidance cut or a large block deal: still hold to the rule. The test is of the rule,
  not of the news.
