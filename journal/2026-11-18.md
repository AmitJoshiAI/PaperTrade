A credit structure receives the net credit; max loss = (strike gap - credit) x units + entry charges (condor: the wider side's gap minus the total credit); max gain = credit x units - entry charges. **Margin**: the engine blocks strike gap x units for every short leg (a condor blocks both sides, more than its max loss) and releases it when the short is closed. **PASS/FAIL** is the engine's own check (derivbook.check_open) run on a copy of the book for every leg, in the order the engine fills them (BUY covers first, then SELLs): per-order risk <= 10% of equity (premium for a long leg, margin for a short leg), free cash, free position slots (a condor takes 4), circuit breaker, expiry after the fill day. A labelled row still passes; its credit may exist only on paper. To exit, CLOSE every leg by its position id (the engine then closes the short first; without ids a long written first is rejected and dropped).
